→ Why this network exists

The average youth athlete quits after 2.86 years.

That is the number we are fighting (Aspen Institute, State of Play). Every org that loses a kid at year three loses the story that would have kept them: the highlight, the profile, the moment that made them feel seen. Brandlete gives athletes a home for that story and gives orgs a reason kids stay. Playmakers are the people who carry that message to the next org, the next coach, the next family, one real conversation at a time.

→ The playbook

How to actually get referrals.

Lead with a question
"How are you keeping kids engaged past the second season?" opens more doors than "Check out this platform."
Use the anchor
Name the one specific thing you know about their org. It signals you actually see them.
Cite the stat
The 2.86-year retention number reframes it from "another tool" to "the problem you already feel."
Show, don't tell
Point them to a live athlete profile so they see it, not just hear about it.
Label the objection
"You might think it's one more thing to manage, fair. Worth ten minutes to see if it isn't?"
Never fake it
One inauthentic intro costs more credibility than ten good ones earn. If it doesn't feel real, don't send it.
